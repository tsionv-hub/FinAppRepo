# Finance Planner: getting started

**[עברית](START.he.md)**

Finance Planner shows what your money is worth after tax, how much you can spend today, and whether your future plans are covered. Your numbers live in **your own Google Sheet**. Nobody else sees them, not even the person who sent you the app.

---

## Step 1. Take a look (1 minute, no sign-in)

1. Open the app link you received.
2. Tap **Explore with example data**.
3. Look around: move the date slider, tap any account or goal for details.

Nothing is saved. Tap **Exit example** when you're done.

---

## Step 2. Ask to be added (once)

Send your **Gmail address** to the person who shared the app. They add you as a tester. Until then, Google won't let you sign in.

---

## Step 3. Make your sheet (5 minutes, easiest on a computer)

1. In the app, tap **Download the example sheet** (made-up numbers) or **Download an empty template**.
2. Go to **drive.google.com** → **New** → **File upload** → choose the file.
3. Open it, then **File** → **Save as Google Sheets**. *(The app can't read the Excel version.)*
4. Put in your own numbers. Keep the tab names and column titles exactly as they are.

---

## Step 4. Put the app on your phone

**iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

**Android:** open the link in **Chrome** → **⋮** → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**) → **Install**.

From now on, **always open it from the icon**.

---

## Step 5. Connect your sheet

1. In Google Sheets, open your sheet → **Share** → **Copy link**.
2. In the app, paste the link → **Continue**.
3. Tap **Continue with Google** and choose your account.
4. Google says **"Google hasn't verified this app"**. That's expected for a private app: tap **Advanced** → **Go to Finance Planner (unsafe)** → **Continue**.
5. The **Sheet check** shows what was found. When everything has a ✓, tap **Open the planner**.

---

## Every day after that

- Open the icon → **Continue with Google**. You sign in each time on purpose: the app keeps no login on your phone.
- Changed numbers in the sheet? Tap **Reload**.
- Changes under **Assumptions** are "what if" only. The app writes to your sheet **only** when you tap a **Save** button.
- **עברית / English** at the top switches the language.

---

## Something not working?

| You see | Do this |
|---|---|
| Google says **access denied** | You're not added yet: see step 2. |
| **"This file is an Excel file"** | In Google Sheets: **File** → **Save as Google Sheets**, and use the new link. |
| **"This Google account cannot open the sheet"** | Sign in with the account that owns the sheet. |
| A **✗** in the sheet check | Fix that tab or column name exactly as shown, then **Check again**. |
| The sign-in window doesn't open | Tap **Sign-in test** at the bottom, then **2. Sign in (redirect)**. |
| An old version | Close the app completely (swipe it away) and open it again. The version number is at the bottom of the app. |
